About me

I am Yu Yan (颜宇), also known as Throb7. I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Transportation at the College of Civil Aviation, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, where I am advised by Danwen Bao.

My research focuses on intelligent low-altitude transportation systems, especially the modeling, simulation, risk assessment, and optimization of its operations.

Before beginning my master's, I received bachelor's degrees in Aviation from RMIT University and Transportation (Airport Operation and Management) from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics.

Research interests

  • Low-altitude transportation systems
  • Transportation system modeling and simulation
  • AI for transportation

Current focus

My current interests lie in understanding how complex transportation systems evolve under operational constraints, and in developing simulation-based and AI-assisted methods to support planning, control, and decision-making.
