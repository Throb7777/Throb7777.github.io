About me

I am a master's student in Transportation at the College of Civil Aviation, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics.

My research interests include urban air mobility, UAV operations, transportation system modeling, airspace safety, low-altitude traffic flow simulation, operations research, and AI-enabled transportation optimization.

Research interests

  • Urban air mobility
  • UAV operations and low-altitude airspace systems
  • Transportation system modeling
  • Airspace safety and risk assessment
  • Low-altitude traffic flow simulation
  • Operations research and optimization
  • AI for transportation

Current focus

I am broadly interested in how urban air mobility and UAV-enabled transportation systems can be modeled, evaluated, and optimized under operational constraints. My current work connects traffic simulation, network optimization, safety assessment, and data-driven decision support.
