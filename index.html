About me

I am Yu Yan (颜宇), also known as Throb7. I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Transportation at the College of Civil Aviation, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, where I am advised by Danwen Bao.

My research focuses on urban air mobility and UAV-enabled transportation systems. I study how these emerging systems can be modeled, simulated, and optimized for real-world operations.

Before beginning my master's, I received bachelor's degrees in Aviation from RMIT University and Transportation (Airport Operation and Management) from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics.

Research interests

  • Urban air mobility
  • UAV operations and low-altitude transportation systems
  • Transportation system modeling and simulation
  • Operations research and network optimization
  • AI-enabled transportation

Current focus

My current research investigates the modeling and optimization of urban air mobility and UAV-enabled transportation under real-world operational constraints. I am particularly interested in combining simulation and operations research with AI methods, including reinforcement learning and large language models, to support planning and decision-making in emerging transportation systems.
